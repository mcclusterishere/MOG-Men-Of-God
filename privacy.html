PEOPLE OF GOD

Privacy Notice

Effective August 27, 2026

The current iPhone, iMessage, Android, and web MVPs have no account, advertising, analytics, tracking, or People of God backend.

Information kept on your device

The app stores only an onboarding-completion flag, local participation counters, and the time of your latest activity so it can show “Showing Up” progress. Detailed feelings, support selections, prayer text, and affirmations are not persisted.

Information you choose to message

When you use the Messages extension, only the response or support request you deliberately approve is placed into the iMessage card. If you choose “Keep my answers private,” no card is inserted. Apple and the people in your conversation handle and can retain the messages you send under their own settings and policies.

On Android and the web, People of God opens your device's Share interface only when you choose to share an invitation, support request, prayer request, or affirmation. The destination service you choose handles that message under its own settings and policies.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or use mental-health information for advertising.
  • We do not provide a pastor, leader, or administrator surveillance dashboard.
  • We do not combine check-in information with HERE, music, CRM, or marketing data.
  • We do not diagnose, treat, or provide emergency services.

Safety

If you or someone else may be unsafe in the United States, call or text 988. Call 911 for immediate danger.

Future versions

If a later version adds accounts, cloud sync, or a backend, this notice and the in-app consent experience must be updated before those features collect information.
