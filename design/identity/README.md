# Men of God Design Identity

This directory is the canonical visual staging area for the Men of God product.

## Brand premise
Men of God is a cross-church brotherhood and platform for Christian men. The visual system should feel forged, machined, engraved and premium, not like a generic dark-mode social app and not like conventional church clip art.

## Material language
- Blackened steel and gunmetal are the primary surfaces.
- Forged antique gold is reserved for status, achievement, Talents and active states.
- Bone/platinum supports readable text and contrast.
- Interfaces should suggest physical manufacture: engraved labels, recessed navigation wells, milled edges, stamped seals and machined progress tracks.
- Motion may use restrained specular shimmer across dark metal. Avoid neon, glassmorphism, generic gradients and SaaS-dashboard styling.

## Product direction
The phone app remains the primary surface. Current reference direction combines the restraint and progress hierarchy of Apple Fitness / FitPlan with a vertically consumable social feed, private-fraternity energy and a much more custom metal identity.

Use the supplied identity assets as source material. Do not replace them with emoji, generic icon libraries, random crosses, lions, crowns or stock church graphics. Simplify or derive production-size variants only when necessary for legibility.
